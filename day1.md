Curriculum architecture

The default progression follows a structured path:

    Engineering Fundamentals (recommended: 4 months)
    Soft Landing (recommended: 6 months)
        Core Systems: shared engineering foundations for all roles
        Specialization Tracks: role-based depth leading to entry-level readiness

Time estimates assume focused study. Part-time learners should expect longer. The key is not speed - the key is consistency in learning and building.
